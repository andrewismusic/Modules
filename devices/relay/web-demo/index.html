    Button combinations

    Keys

    • Random 1: new effects
    • Random 2: new settings and fader ranges
    • Bypass: on or off
    • Keys act when you let go

    With Shift

    • Shift + Random 1: save (dial picks the slot)
    • Shift + Random 2: load
    • Shift + Bypass: menu (opens straight away)
    • In menus: dial scrolls, Bypass enters or confirms, Shift goes back or cancels

    Dial and fader

    • Fader: brings the four effects in across its travel; the first 3% is dry
    • Dial: turn clockwise to move every setting toward its own destination

    Computer keyboard

    • Z Shift, X Random 1, C Random 2, V Bypass
    • Arrows scroll menus
    • Space: play or stop
