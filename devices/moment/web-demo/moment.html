Button combinations

Encoders

  • Turn: set the value (this also becomes the value Random works around)
  • Tap: show and latch the second parameter (pink): quant on Pitch, fade on Length
  • Hold Shift: show all second parameters
  • Press and hold, then turn: adjust the other layer for a moment
  • Bypass + tap: reset to default
  • Direction / Thin follows Spec · Loop: Direction in Loop, Thin in Spectral

Random

  • Random: every included parameter moves within its range around your value (Random glide sets how long the moves take)
  • Random + tap: include or exclude a parameter (included ones breathe toward amber)
  • Hold Random: your values with their ranges in amber
  • Random + turn: that parameter's range
  • Random + Bypass: all ranges full

Freeze and settings

  • Freeze acts when pressed; the Latch / Mom switch decides tap on and off, or frozen while held (the Freeze jack follows it)
  • Shift + Freeze: fresh capture
  • Random + Freeze: let Random freeze or release
  • Man / Roll switch: a freeze holds, or re-captures every loop
  • Shift + Bypass: settings. Press or turn an encoder: unipolar or bipolar range. Bypass keeps, Shift cancels

CV and computer keyboard

  • In CV assignment mode: tap controls to assign, tap Random for random assignments, tap Bypass to clear the jack, hold Random or Bypass 0.8 s to assign that key
  • Z Shift, X Freeze, C Random, V Bypass
  • Hold a key and click an encoder for combinations
  • Space: play or stop
